# trophy-r01-B: six plaques, one per grandkid

Same chat as trophy-r01-A. Like the six kid fish: one family, each a little different, so every
grandkid gets their own. Claude slices the sheet with designs/tools/slice_sheet.py.

```
Using the plaque above as the style guide (same wood, outline weight and colors), make a sheet of 6 small wooden trophy plaques arranged in a 3 by 2 grid with plenty of empty space between them. Mix the shapes: shield, oval, and rounded arch. Each has a different simple symbol in the middle where a mount would go: a red heart, a gold star, an acorn, a little green pine tree, a deer hoof track, and a feather. Each has the same small blank brass nameplate at the bottom with no engraving. Cute and chubby rounded shapes, bold even dark-brown outline, flat colors with only light soft shading. Fully transparent background, no wall, no shadow, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no border. Crisp, clean edges, ready to print on a white coffee mug. Highest quality.
```

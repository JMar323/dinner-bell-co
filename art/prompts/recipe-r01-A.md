# recipe-r01-A: Nana's Recipe Card, the blank card

Mug 2 of batch 1, "Nana's Recipe Card" (2026-W41 idea #4). The card carries all the words as Printify
text layers: "Recipe for a Full Heart", one ingredient line per grandkid ("1 heaping cup of Emma"),
"Stir with hugs, bake low and slow, serve with cocoa", "Served daily in Nana's kitchen".
So the card must be blank. Same house look as the fishing mug (bold dark-brown outline, flat colors).
New chat. Attach the kid fish sheet (limit-r02-B) only as a style guide for line and color.

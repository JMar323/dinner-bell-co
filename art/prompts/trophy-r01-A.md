# trophy-r01-A: Papa's Trophy Wall, one plaque

Mug 3 of batch 1, "Papa's Trophy Wall" (2026-W41 idea #1, hunting line). Each grandkid gets a wooden
trophy plaque with a blank brass nameplate (the name is a Printify text layer); a heart sits where a
mount would go, so the kids are his best trophies. Header "Papa's Best Trophies" and "Not one of them
is for sale." are text layers too. Claude lays out one plaque per name, so 3 to 6 grandkids all fit.
New chat. Attach the kid fish sheet (limit-r02-B) only as a style guide for line and color.

```
Use the attached image only as a guide for the drawing style (outline weight and flat coloring); do not draw any fish. Draw a single wooden shield-shaped trophy plaque, the kind a hunter hangs on the cabin wall, seen straight on. Warm honey-brown wood with a few simple grain lines and a thin darker beveled edge. Where a mount would go, a plump red heart sits in the middle of the plaque. At the bottom, a small blank brass nameplate with two tiny screws, completely blank with no engraving. Cute and chubby rounded shapes, bold even dark-brown outline, flat colors with only light soft shading. Warm, rustic and a little sweet. The plaque fills about 85% of the frame. Fully transparent background, no wall, no shadow, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no border. Crisp, clean edges, ready to print on a white coffee mug. Square image, highest quality.
```
